My name is Henry Schiller.

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in the Department of Philosophy at King's College London.

henry.schiller [at] kcl.ac.uk

My current research is about the nature of learning, anticipation, and desire, and the role that these things play in how we can locate ourselves in time. More generally, I have ongoing research projects that aim to further our understanding of rationality, linguistic communication, and social cognition.
