My name is Henry Schiller.

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in the Department of Philosophy at King's College London.

henry.schiller [at] kcl.ac.uk

My current research is about the aspects of human human thought that render the world intelligible to us in the way that it is. (I'm especially interested in the role that learning, anticipation, and desire play in how we understand ourselves as temporally located.) More generally, I have research interests and projects that aim to further our understanding of rational thought, linguistic communication, and social cognition.
